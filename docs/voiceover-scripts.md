## VISUAL DIRECTION NOTES (All Platforms)

- **Screen record the actual platform** — scroll the homepage slowly, click through tabs, show the sign-up page
- **Use game footage or hero cards** wherever possible for the hooks
- **Keep text overlays clean** — white text, slight drop shadow, bottom third of screen
- **Music:** Epic orchestral or anime-style soundtrack, low volume under the voiceover
- **Pacing:** Quick cuts during the showcase sections, slower during the sign-up walkthrough and CTA
- **End card:** Always show the full URL clearly on screen for 3-5 seconds at the end
- **Captions:** Add captions to all videos — most viewers watch without sound initially
